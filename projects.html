ALL MY PROJECTS!

AI in Newsrooms (Mentorship, 2025)

Month-long study at The Indian Express on responsibly using AI to speed newsroom workflows: summarization, SEO, video upscaling, and synthetic voice. Mapped pipelines and limits; recommended training on Indian data and journalist workshops.

More

Tools explored: ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Gemini, Perplexity, Meta AI, Runway, Luma.ai, ElevenLabs. Key insight: AI can’t replace editors, but it can optimize repetitive tasks and enhance storytelling quality when adopted responsibly.

EMG-Controlled Robotic Hand

Built in the YTS program at Plaksha University (2022). EMG sensors captured muscle signals → Arduino processed thresholds → servos actuated origami-inspired joints. Lightweight, low-cost prototype with proportional response.

More

Learned biosignal filtering, calibration, and human-machine interaction; potential for rehab/prosthetics translating intent into motion.

Fake-News Detector

LSTM model (TensorFlow) trained on labeled Kaggle data. Tokenization + embeddings feed sequence model to predict credibility with probability output, aiding verification and digital literacy.

More

LSTM’s gated architecture retains context; model trained to minimize cross-entropy. Strengthened skills in preprocessing and ethical AI for misinformation.

Desktop Gardening Assistant

Client app (Python Tkinter + SQLite) for plant care: per-plant notes, reminders (watering, fertilizing, pruning), photo logs, and seasonal tips. Improves consistency and organization for hobbyists.

More

Interview-driven design; local database avoids cloud dependency; lightweight UI for non-technical users.

Python Tools for News

Three parts: Hacker News scraper (Requests + BeautifulSoup), sentiment analyzer (TextBlob), and LSTM fake-news checker. Automates curation, tone analysis, and verification.

More

Shows a pipeline from collection → interpretation → credibility assessment; emphasizes transparency and accuracy.

Smart-Home Automations

Alexa routines + sensors for comfort and energy savings: motion-based lights, time-based thermostat, and custom voice commands. Demonstrates event-triggered, condition-action loops with safe overrides.

More

Hands-on with IoT protocols and user-centric automation; integrates natural-language control with environmental sensors.
